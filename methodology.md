# Methodology

## Customer Segmentation Approach

This project applies RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) analysis to classify Olist customers based on purchasing behavior.

## RFM Metrics

### Recency
Measures how recently customers made their latest purchase.

### Frequency
Measures the number of purchases made by customers.

### Monetary
Measures total spending generated by customers.


## Customer Segments

Customers were classified into groups including:

- Champions
- Loyalty Customers
- Cannot Lose Them
- At Risk
- About To Sleep
- Lost Customers
## Business Objective

The objective is to identify valuable customers, detect churn risks and support customer retention strategies.
