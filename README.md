# Olist Customer Segmentation Analysis


## Project Overview

This project analyzes customer behavior from Olist, the largest department store marketplace in Brazil.

The objective is to identify valuable customers, understand churn behavior and discover retention opportunities using RFM customer segmentation.


## Business Problem

Olist wants to understand:

- Who are the most valuable customers?
- Which customers are at risk of leaving?
- How can customer retention strategies be improved?


## Tools Used

- Power BI
- Data Analytics
- RFM Analysis
- Data Visualization


## Methodology

Customers were segmented using:

- Recency
- Frequency
- Monetary Value


## Key Insights


### Best Customers

Champions and Loyalty customers were identified as high-value segments.

Champions:
- 6 customers
- 20 orders
- Approximately $1.86K revenue

Loyal customers:
- 17 customers
- Approximately $6.4K revenue


### Churn Analysis

Lost customers generated significant historical revenue despite no longer purchasing.

### At-Risk Customers

Segments such as "Cannot Lose Them", "At Risk" and "About To Sleep" require retention strategies.
